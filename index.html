What can leave the phone — and only if you switch it on

CargoLog works on its own and sends nothing by itself. There is a diagnostic channel to a private server run by the developer, so that failures can be looked at on real data instead of guessed from a description. It has three independent switches, and all three are off until you turn them on:

  • Application activity logs — to see errors and failures on real phones
  • The trip database — to see what trips look like in real work
  • Container number photos — to teach the scanner to read dirty and dented numbers

Each switch works on its own and can be switched off at any moment; while all three are off, nothing is sent at all. Full detail in the Privacy Policy.

What CargoLog does

Trip Logging

Record every trip from origin to destination. Track odometer readings, departure and arrival times, waiting time, loading and unloading. Support for multi-leg trips — park at a stop, continue the same delivery the next day.

Cargo Tracking

Supports absolutely all types of transport and trailers, including operations with cranes and manipulators.

Shift Management

Start and end shifts. See your total work time, number of trips, and kilometers driven. Shift history grouped by date with visual separators.

Garage

Manage your trucks and trailers. Track vehicle type, plate number, year, body type. Switch between vehicles. Support for Semi Trucks and Rigid trucks with different trailer configurations.

Refueling Log

Record every refuel — liters, odometer, truck plate. Generate PDF refueling reports.

Timers

Built-in timers for waiting at client, refueling, unloading — with background notifications so you can close the app and get alerted.

Container OCR

Scan container numbers with your camera — fully offline, on your phone, no internet. Automatic ISO 6346 check digit validation with haptic feedback. Vertical (top-to-bottom column) scanning is now in beta.

Document Storage

Store documents for each vehicle — registration, insurance, inspection. Driver's own documents sit alongside truck and trailer papers, with reminders before a paper runs out.
