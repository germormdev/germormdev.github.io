What CargoLog does

Trip Logging

Odometer, departure and arrival times, waiting, loading. Support for multi-leg trips and overnight stops.

Container OCR

Scan container (ISO 6346) and seal numbers with your camera. Automatic check digit validation.

Garage & Shifts

Manage trucks and trailers. Start and end shifts. Full work history grouped by date.

Refueling Log

Record every refuel: liters, odometer, truck plate. Generate convenient PDF reports.

Smart Timers

Built-in timers for waiting at client, refueling, and unloading with background notifications.

Fully Offline

No internet needed during your shift. No accounts. Your trip data never leaves your phone.
