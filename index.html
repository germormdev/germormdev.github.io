What CargoLog does

Trip Logging

Record every trip from origin to destination. Track odometer readings, departure and arrival times, waiting time, loading and unloading. Support for multi-leg trips — park at a stop, continue the same delivery the next day.

Cargo Tracking

Supports absolutely all types of transport and trailers, including operations with cranes and manipulators.

Shift Management

Start and end shifts. See your total work time, number of trips, and kilometers driven. Shift history grouped by date with visual separators.

Garage

Manage your trucks and trailers. Track vehicle type, plate number, year, body type. Switch between vehicles. Support for Semi Trucks and Rigid trucks with different trailer configurations.

Refueling Log

Record every refuel — liters, odometer, truck plate. Generate PDF refueling reports.

Timers

Built-in timers for waiting at client, refueling, unloading — with background notifications so you can close the app and get alerted.

Container OCR

Scan container and seal numbers with your camera. Automatic ISO 6346 check digit validation with haptic feedback.
